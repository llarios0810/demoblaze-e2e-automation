Conclusions:

- The purchase flow on Demoblaze was successfully automated.

- The test validates adding products to the cart, viewing the cart, and completing the purchase process.

- Cypress provided an efficient and straightforward way to automate real user interactions in the browser.
- It was identified that Demoblaze uses browser alerts when products are added to the shopping cart.

- End-to-end automation helps detect failures in critical business workflows before deployment.
